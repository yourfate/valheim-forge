Forge Roll Predictor

Valheim's Forge of Potential doesn't roll fresh dice. Every result comes from a fixed sequence seeded by your character's name, the item and the target level. Pick an item to see what's coming.

Fills in your name, your item levels and the attempts you've already made. The file is read in this browser only. On Linux with Steam it's in ~/.local/share/Steam/userdata/<id>/892970/remote/characters/.

Each idol: 65% success. Any failure destroys the item (it never just downgrades).

Roll sequence per target level

Each row is its own sequence. The first cell is your next try at that level; the following cells are the tries after it, including ones made with other copies of the same item. Rolls at or below 0.650 succeed. A dashed outline means the roll is within 0.001 of the threshold.

Your refinable items

Only the idol is spent at the forge. When an item breaks you get back 35% (rounded up) of its base materials plus the materials for its previous upgrade step. Idols aren't refunded.

Without a save loaded, every level is assumed untried. Tries you've made at a level move its sequence forward, so load your save if you've used the forge before. Reloading without saving doesn't change a result: the same roll comes up again.
